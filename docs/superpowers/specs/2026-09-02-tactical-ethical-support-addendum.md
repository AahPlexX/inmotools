# Tactical Tooling — Ethical Monetization & Micro-Tipping Addendum

**As of:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** Binding requirement for any future InmoTools tactical/sports workspace. No sports-specific capability or success message may be surfaced until the corresponding feature actually exists and completes successfully.

## 7. Ethical Monetization & Micro-Tipping Integrations

### Zero-paywall guarantee

Every tactical capability is to remain fully usable without authentication, advertising, export watermarks, or feature paywalls. This guarantee includes, when implemented, high-resolution WebCodecs video export, Voronoi pitch analytics, printable vector drill/PDF playbooks, 4v4 through 11v11 pitch formats, and multi-phase keyframing.

This addendum does **not** assert that those tactical capabilities currently exist in this repository. It governs them when a tactical workspace implementing them is added.

### Contextual support prompts

High-value completed actions may emit the shared `inmotools:support-prompt` browser event. The prompt must be non-modal, dismissible, accessible, and must never block or degrade the completed output.

For a completed tactical animation export matching the described workflow, the approved copy is:

> Rendered full 1080p 60 FPS tactical animation locally. If this upgraded your team's match preparation, support independent sports tooling with a coffee.

Equivalent truthful copy may be used for other completed high-value tactical actions, such as a multi-page coaching PDF playbook export. Copy must describe the action that actually completed; it may not advertise unavailable functionality.

### Persistent support action

The global InmoTools chrome must keep a persistent, accessible `☕ Buy me a coffee ($3)` link to the existing support endpoint. Support is optional and must never change tool capability, export quality, limits, privacy, or access.
